Elevated sST2 After COVID-19

Analysis transparency for a study in patients with cardiovascular comorbidities

What this page is

This is a companion transparency page for the manuscript "Elevated Soluble ST2 Three Months After COVID-19 Infection in Patients With Cardiovascular Comorbidities." It documents how the reported statistics were produced, and lets you explore the published summary results interactively, independently of the paper text.

What is included

SPSS syntaxMethod reconstructionInteractive figuresSupplementary analysis
  • A complete, reconstructed IBM SPSS Statistics syntax file covering normality testing, group comparisons (ANOVA/Kruskal–Wallis with post-hoc pairwise tests), correlation analysis, and the multivariable linear regression reported in the manuscript.
  • A second syntax file plus a short Python script covering the manuscript's Supplementary Statistical Analysis: confounder-adjusted (ANCOVA) group comparison, regression diagnostics and a log-transformed sensitivity model, a restricted-cubic-spline nonlinearity check, a logistic-regression/ROC threshold analysis, correlation with additional biomarkers, and a multiple-imputation sensitivity analysis.
  • Interactive charts and a sortable table below, built directly from the group-level summary statistics reported in the manuscript's Tables 1–3 and its Supplementary Statistical Analysis.
  • The syntax is reconstructed from the Methods section of the manuscript for transparency and reproducibility of the statistical approach — it mirrors the described plan step by step.

What is deliberately not included

  • No patient-level or raw data. The study involves clinical data collected under ethics approval (National Cardiovascular Centre Harapan Kita Hospital Medicine Ethics Committee), and individual records are not shared here. Every number on this page is a group-level summary statistic already reported in the manuscript.
  • No identifying information of any kind.

Supplementary Statistical Analysis

The six sections below extend the primary results with confounder adjustment, model diagnostics, nonlinearity and threshold checks, additional biomarker correlations, and a missing-data sensitivity analysis. Every figure uses group-level summary statistics from supplementary_summary_stats.json, reconstructed from the manuscript's Supplementary Statistical Analysis document.

S1 · Confounder-adjusted group comparison (ANCOVA)

Estimated marginal means of sST2 adjusted for age, sex, DM, hypertension, CAD, and obesity. F(2,153) = 16.72, p < 0.001, partial η² = 0.18. Every pairwise difference remained significant after Bonferroni correction.

S2 · Regression diagnostics

Variance inflation factors for the Table 3 predictor set. All values stay well below the common threshold of 5, indicating no meaningful multicollinearity.

S3 · Is the PaO₂/Ct relationship linear?

Restricted cubic spline (3 df), predicted sST2 with 95% CI, each holding the other variable at its median. A flat-then-declining curve would suggest the association is not strictly linear.

S4 · ROC threshold analysis

Predicting a high sST2 concentration (upper quartile, >2840.9 pg/mL) from admission PaO₂ and Ct value. Youden-optimal cutoffs: PaO₂ ≤ 87.8 mmHg, Ct ≤ 18.4.

S5 · Correlation with additional biomarkers

Spearman correlation with sST2 among case-group patients with available values. None reach significance, consistent with excluding these biomarkers from the primary model.

S6 · Multiple imputation sensitivity analysis

Complete-case vs. multiple-imputation (MICE, 30 imputations) coefficients after adding hs-CRP, D-dimer, NT-proBNP, and troponin back into the model. PaO₂ and Ct value stay independently significant either way.
